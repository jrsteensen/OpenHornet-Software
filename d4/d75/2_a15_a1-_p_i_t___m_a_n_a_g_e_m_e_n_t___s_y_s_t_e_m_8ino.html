◆ ISR()

ISR ( PCINT0_vect )

Power Supply "Failed State" Function.

This function provides realtime feedback from the ATX Power Supply. In the event of a failed PSU, this function will trigger on the loss of the PWR_OK signal from the PSU. The triggered interupt disables the PSU to assist in protecting the simulator from an unhealthy PSU state.
